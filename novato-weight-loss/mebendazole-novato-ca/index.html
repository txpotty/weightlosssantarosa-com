    How Mebendazole Treatment Works in Novato, CA

    How Mebendazole Works in Novato

    Mebendazole blocks a worm's ability to take in glucose by disrupting tiny structures inside its cells called microtubules. Without energy, the worms die. They pass in the stool over the following days, and most people never notice them, which is normal and does not mean the drug failed. Very little of the drug reaches the bloodstream. In the US, it is sold as an FDA-approved chewable tablet.

    Who Mebendazole Is For

    Adults usually ask for it because of pinworm. Nighttime anal itching, especially when kids at home have the same complaint, is the classic sign. It also treats roundworm, whipworm and hookworm, which are more often linked to travel or certain environments. Adults with a positive test qualify. It is not a detox and has no role in weight loss.

    Mebendazole Dosing and Schedule in Novato

    For pinworm, the usual plan is a single 100 mg chewable tablet, repeated in two weeks. That second dose catches worms that hatched from eggs the first dose could not reach. Other intestinal worms are often treated with 100 mg twice daily for three days, followed by a stool test in some cases. Chew it fully. You can take it with or without food.

    What to Expect After Taking Mebendazole

    Most people feel nothing after the dose. A few notice mild cramping or loose stools for a day. Itching usually eases within a few days, and it can take another week for the skin to calm down completely, especially if it was scratched a lot. You may not see worms. The repeat dose at week two is the step people forget, so set a reminder.

    Side Effects of Mebendazole in Novato

    Because so little is absorbed, mebendazole is well tolerated at short courses. Belly pain, loose stools and nausea are the usual complaints. Rash is rare. Extended high-dose use for other purposes can affect blood counts and the liver, so this program uses only standard short courses. Message your provider about a rash, fever or severe stomach pain.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your intake asks about symptoms, timing, household members, recent travel and any test results. Pregnancy is a key question. Mebendazole is generally avoided in the first trimester, and the provider also screens for breastfeeding, liver disease and interacting drugs. Taking metronidazole at the same time has been tied to rare but serious skin reactions. Non-pinworm infections usually need a stool test first.

    How Mebendazole Compares to Other Options in Novato

    Albendazole works the same way and is sometimes used for the same infections. Over-the-counter pyrantel pamoate is another pinworm option that paralyzes the worms rather than starving them. Ivermectin is preferred for strongyloides, scabies and lice, rather than pinworm. When the type of parasite is still unclear, the antiparasitic protocol starts with that question and may recommend testing. Your provider recommends the drug with the best evidence.
