    What Causes Low Libido in Novato

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Antidepressants, the pill, perimenopause, thyroid issues, stress, bad sleep, painful sex and relationship tension all show up often. So does how you feel in your body. When low desire is persistent, distressing and not better explained by another cause, it may be diagnosed as hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. It often helps women whose interest changed after starting a medication, having a baby, entering perimenopause or going through a stressful stretch at work or home. No diagnosis is needed first. If lower desire does not bother you, there is nothing to fix, because distress is part of the definition. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in Novato

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. PT-141, brand name Vyleesi, is a 1.75 mg self-injection taken at least 45 minutes before sex, with limits of once a day and eight times a month. Oxytocin is a compounded option. It is used off-label as a spray or troche before intimacy and is not FDA-approved. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Early steps often include adjusting a medication, treating dryness or improving sleep, sometimes alongside a new prescription. PT-141 can work with the first dose. Flibanserin builds over four to eight weeks, and providers usually stop it at eight weeks if nothing has improved. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in Novato

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Those effects are worse with alcohol or CYP3A4-blocking drugs, which is why the dose goes in at bedtime and the label limits drinking near that dose. Nausea is common with PT-141. It can also cause flushing, headache, injection site reactions, a brief rise in blood pressure and darker skin patches with repeated use. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. It is also avoided with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors such as fluconazole, clarithromycin or some HIV medications, and alcohol must be limited near bedtime. Blood pressure matters for PT-141. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in Novato

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It tends to suit women who rarely drink and prefer one routine pill, though the average benefit in trials was modest, around one extra satisfying sexual event per month compared with placebo. PT-141 is as-needed. That suits some women, but nausea puts others off. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
