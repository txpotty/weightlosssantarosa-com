    How GLP-1 Microdosing Works in Novato

    Microdosing is a dosing strategy, not a separate drug. It means taking semaglutide or tirzepatide at doses lower than the standard labeled schedule, or increasing the dose more slowly, while the medicine still works on the same gut hormone receptors. Others notice little change. Because brand pens come in fixed doses, low-dose plans often use compounded medication, which is not FDA-approved.

    Who GLP-1 Microdosing Is For

    Three groups ask about it most. The typical candidates had trouble with nausea on standard doses, prefer a gentler start or are trying to hold onto weight they already lost. It does not bypass medical criteria. Anti-aging and anti-inflammation claims lack solid research behind them.

    Low-Dose Schedule in Novato

    There is no single standard schedule. A common approach begins at a fraction of the labeled starting dose, like part of 0.25 mg semaglutide or 2.5 mg tirzepatide, and moves up in smaller increments. Most plans stay weekly. Any change should go through your provider first.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Early weeks vary a lot between people. At low doses, one person may feel full sooner by week two while another feels no difference until the dose is nudged up. Nausea is usually milder. Expect modest, gradual change.

    Side Effects in Novato

    Lower doses reduce side effects but do not erase them. Nausea, constipation, diarrhea, reflux and reduced appetite are still the most common, especially in the days after any dose increase, even a small one. The boxed warning remains. GLP-1 risks like pancreatitis and gallstones, and the rodent thyroid tumor warning, do not go away at low doses.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening is the same as for full doses. Your provider reviews your BMI, weight history, conditions, medications and any past GLP-1 use, including the highest dose you reached and why you stopped. Thyroid cancer history rules it out. Pregnancy, pancreatitis, gastroparesis and eating disorders all need careful review.

    Microdosing Compared With Standard GLP-1 Treatment in Novato

    Standard dosing has the trial data. Wegovy and Zepbound at full doses were tested in large clinical trials that showed average weight loss of about 15 to 20 percent, while microdosing has not been studied at that scale. Expect smaller average results. Some people use a low dose for maintenance after reaching a goal.
