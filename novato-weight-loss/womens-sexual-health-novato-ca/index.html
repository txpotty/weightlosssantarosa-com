    Understanding Low Desire in Novato

    Low sexual desire is one of the most common sexual health concerns women report. When it is persistent, causes distress and is not explained by another condition or medication, it may be diagnosed as hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. More than one factor is common. SSRIs, birth control, perimenopause, thyroid issues, pain with sex, sleep and relationship stress all play roles. A good evaluation looks at each.

    Low Libido Treatment for Women

    Treatment starts with finding what changed. Your provider reviews medications known to lower desire, asks about menopause symptoms, thyroid history and pain with sex, and looks at sleep and stress. Sometimes the answer is small. Addressing dryness or changing an antidepressant with your prescriber sometimes helps more than a new prescription. Medication options follow if needed.

    Flibanserin for Women in Novato

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is FDA-approved for premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. It is a non-hormonal 100 mg tablet taken once daily at bedtime that acts on serotonin and dopamine in the brain. It is not an as-needed drug. Providers usually reassess at eight weeks and stop it if there is no benefit. Alcohol timing matters with flibanserin.

    PT-141 for Women

    PT-141, or bremelanotide, is FDA-approved as Vyleesi for premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. It is a 1.75 mg injection under the skin of the belly or thigh, given at least 45 minutes before sex, and it acts on melanocortin receptors in the brain linked to desire. Expect some nausea early. Use it no more than once in 24 hours or eight times a month. Compounded versions are not FDA-approved.

    Oxytocin Therapy in Novato

    Oxytocin is a hormone involved in bonding, touch and arousal. Compounded oxytocin is sometimes prescribed as a nasal spray or troche taken before intimacy to support arousal and closeness. This use is off-label. Compounded oxytocin is not FDA-approved for this purpose, research is limited and results vary widely. Your provider explains the evidence honestly.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Options

    Menopausal status comes first. Flibanserin and Vyleesi are approved for premenopausal women, so postmenopausal women may be offered off-label options, hormonal approaches or a referral instead. Then comes routine. Regular drinkers often lean toward PT-141, women with blood pressure issues toward flibanserin, and liver function shapes the choice too. Your preferences count as well.

    Side Effects and Safety in Novato

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea and low blood pressure. Those effects are stronger with alcohol or with drugs that block CYP3A4, which is why bedtime dosing and alcohol limits are part of the label. Nausea is common with PT-141. Flushing, headache, a short blood pressure rise and skin darkening with frequent use are also possible. Oxytocin can cause headache or nasal irritation.
