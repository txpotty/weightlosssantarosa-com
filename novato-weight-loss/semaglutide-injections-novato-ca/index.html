    How Semaglutide Works in Novato

    Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist, which means it copies a hormone the gut sends out after a meal. The signal reaches appetite centers in the brain, while the stomach holds food a little longer. Cravings usually ease. Because the molecule is built to resist breakdown, it lasts about a week in the body, which is why a single injection covers seven full days. It also lowers post-meal blood sugar, which is why Ozempic treats type 2 diabetes.

    Who Semaglutide Is For

    Adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with a weight-related condition, are the usual candidates. Sleep apnea, prediabetes, high cholesterol and high blood pressure are the conditions providers see most. It is not for type 1 diabetes. Adults with established heart disease may benefit beyond weight, since Wegovy is approved to reduce major cardiovascular events in that group. The provider weighs all of this against your full health history before deciding.

    Dosing and the Weekly Schedule in Novato

    The standard Wegovy ladder starts at 0.25 mg weekly, then climbs through four more monthly steps before reaching 2.4 mg. Each step usually lasts four weeks. Inject on the same day every week, at any time, with or without food. The shot goes under the skin of the belly or thigh, and moving the spot each week cuts down on redness and small bumps. Five days is the window for a late dose, and after that you wait for the next scheduled shot.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Weeks one to four at 0.25 mg are a warm-up, so modest appetite changes are normal and not a sign it is failing. Nausea often shows up in the first two days after a shot. The 1 mg step is a common turning point. Around month three, portion sizes tend to drop on their own and late-night snacking fades. In STEP 1, weight loss kept building through week 60 before leveling off, so the first eight weeks are only a small part of the picture.

    Side Effects of Semaglutide in Novato

    Nausea, diarrhea, constipation and vomiting are the most reported side effects, and they tend to flare in the week after a step up. Eating smaller meals and stopping at the first sign of fullness helps. Hydrate well. Less common risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, kidney problems from dehydration and low blood sugar when semaglutide is paired with insulin or a sulfonylurea. The label carries a boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The intake asks about a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2, which rules semaglutide out entirely. Your provider also looks for past pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, diabetic retinopathy, severe stomach emptying problems and any history of an eating disorder before writing a prescription. Pregnancy rules it out. Recent labs such as an A1c or a kidney panel help but are not always required.

    Tirzepatide, sold as Zepbound, produced larger average weight loss than semaglutide in the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, about 20 percent versus 14 percent at 72 weeks. What semaglutide offers is more years of safety data and a heart-protection approval for Wegovy. A daily semaglutide tablet exists for patients who would rather not inject.
