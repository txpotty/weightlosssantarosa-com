    How the Antiparasitic Protocol Works in Napa

    The protocol starts with your symptoms and possible exposures, not with a pill. Your provider considers which parasites are realistic given your travel, pets, food and water, plus anyone at home with the same symptoms. Testing comes next when it would help. You may be asked to get a stool ova and parasite test or a specific antigen test before treatment. Treatment uses prescription anti-parasitics with established uses, and a follow-up confirms symptoms are gone.

    Who This Protocol Is For

    Adults with symptoms or exposures that raise a fair concern for a parasite are the right fit. The most common case is nighttime anal itching in a home with school-age children, which usually points to pinworm. Others have traveled somewhere intestinal worms are common or already have a positive test from another provider. It is not a detox. It has no role in weight loss either.

    Dosing and Schedule in Napa

    The schedule depends on the drug and the parasite. Pinworm is commonly treated with one dose of mebendazole 100 mg or albendazole 400 mg, repeated after two weeks to catch worms that hatched after the first dose. Ivermectin is dosed by weight. It is usually given as one dose or a short course of one to two days. Your provider writes out exact dates for every dose.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Most courses take one to three days, sometimes with a repeat dose two weeks later. A day or two of mild stomach upset or tiredness after dosing is common. With pinworm, the itching usually eases within a few days, and the remaining irritation can take another week to settle. Week two is the repeat dose. A check or a follow-up test may be suggested about a month after treatment.

    Side Effects and Precautions in Napa

    Nausea, abdominal pain, diarrhea and headache are the side effects people mention most often. Ivermectin can occasionally cause rash or itching, especially as parasites die off. Mebendazole and albendazole are poorly absorbed into the blood when used for intestinal worms, which keeps most side effects mild at short courses. Pregnancy changes things. Some anti-parasitics are avoided in pregnancy, so tell your provider if there is any chance.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your intake asks when symptoms started, where you have traveled in the past year and whether pets or family members are involved. Prior test results help a lot. The provider checks for pregnancy and liver disease and looks for drug interactions, including warfarin with ivermectin and metronidazole with mebendazole. Bloody stools, high fever or severe belly pain lead to an in-person referral. Some people are asked to finish testing before any medication is prescribed.

    How the Protocol Compares to Over-the-Counter Cleanses in Napa

    Herbal parasite cleanses sold online are not regulated as medications. Their ingredients and doses vary widely from bottle to bottle, and most have never been tested against a specific infection in people. Prescription drugs come with set dosing. Ivermectin and mebendazole, both offered individually through Santa Rosa Weight Loss, each work best against particular parasites. Your provider picks one drug rather than layering several products.
