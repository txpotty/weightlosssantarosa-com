    Understanding Low Desire in Santa Rosa

    Low sexual desire is one of the most common sexual health concerns women report. When it is persistent, causes distress and is not explained by another condition or medication, it may be diagnosed as hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. More than one factor is common. SSRIs, birth control, perimenopause, thyroid issues, pain with sex, sleep and relationship stress all play roles. A good evaluation looks at each.

    Low Libido Treatment for Women

    Treatment starts with finding what changed. Your provider reviews medications known to lower desire, asks about menopause symptoms, thyroid history and pain with sex, and looks at sleep and stress. Simple fixes come first. Addressing dryness or changing an antidepressant with your prescriber sometimes helps more than a new prescription. Medication options follow if needed.

    Flibanserin for Women in Santa Rosa

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is FDA-approved for premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. The dose is one 100 mg tablet each night at bedtime, and it works on serotonin and dopamine rather than hormones. It is not an as-needed drug. If nothing has improved by about eight weeks, it is typically stopped. Alcohol timing matters with flibanserin.

    PT-141 for Women

    PT-141, or bremelanotide, is FDA-approved as Vyleesi for premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. You inject 1.75 mg under the skin of the abdomen or thigh at least 45 minutes ahead of sex, and it works on brain receptors tied to desire. Nausea is common at first. Use it no more than once in 24 hours or eight times a month. Compounded versions are not FDA-approved.

    Oxytocin Therapy in Santa Rosa

    Oxytocin is a hormone involved in bonding, touch and arousal. Compounded oxytocin is sometimes prescribed as a nasal spray or troche taken before intimacy to support arousal and closeness. This use is off-label. Compounded oxytocin is not FDA-approved for this purpose, research is limited and results vary widely. Your provider explains the evidence honestly.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Options

    Menopausal status comes first. Flibanserin and Vyleesi are approved for premenopausal women, so postmenopausal women may be offered off-label options, hormonal approaches or a referral instead. Daily or as-needed is next. Women who drink regularly may do better with PT-141, while women with high blood pressure may do better with flibanserin, and liver health matters for flibanserin. Your preferences count as well.

    Side Effects and Safety in Santa Rosa

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea and low blood pressure. Alcohol and CYP3A4-blocking drugs make those effects worse, which explains the bedtime dose and drinking rules. PT-141 causes nausea often. Flushing, headache, a short blood pressure rise and skin darkening with frequent use are also possible. Oxytocin can cause headache or nasal irritation.
