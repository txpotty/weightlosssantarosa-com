    How Tadalafil Treatment Works in Petaluma, CA

    How Tadalafil Works in Petaluma

    Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor, the same drug class as sildenafil. Blocking PDE5 keeps more cGMP around, and cGMP relaxes the smooth muscle in penile blood vessels so blood flows in more easily when you are aroused. Its half-life is about 17.5 hours. That is why one dose can last a day and a half. Arousal is still required.

    Who Tadalafil Is For

    Men with ED who want flexibility tend to like tadalafil. The as-needed dose suits men who want a longer window than sildenafil gives, such as a weekend away. Daily dosing suits men who have sex twice a week or more. Men with both ED and urinary symptoms from an enlarged prostate can treat both with one daily 5 mg tablet, since tadalafil is FDA-approved for both conditions. Your provider makes the final call.

    Tadalafil Dosing Options in Petaluma

    As-needed dosing usually starts at 10 mg, taken 30 minutes to two hours before sex. The range runs from 5 mg to 20 mg. Daily tadalafil is 2.5 or 5 mg at about the same time each day, food or no food, reaching a steady level in roughly five days. One dose per day, either way. Food does not slow it much.

    What to Expect in the First Weeks

    With as-needed tadalafil, most men notice a difference on the first or second try. Daily dosing takes about a week to feel steady. The first week or two can bring headaches, heartburn or a dull lower back ache, and those often fade as you keep taking it. Track what you notice. After about a month, your provider reviews how it is going.

    Tadalafil Side Effects in Petaluma

    Headache, indigestion, back pain and muscle aches are the most common side effects. Back aches typically start 12 to 24 hours after taking it and clear within a couple of days. Some men also get a flushed face or a stuffy nose. An erection lasting more than four hours, sudden vision loss or sudden hearing loss is an emergency and needs care right away, not a message. Rare, but real.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates in any form rule out tadalafil, including nitroglycerin and poppers, and so does riociguat. Men with a heart attack in the past 90 days, unstable angina, uncontrolled blood pressure or a stroke in the past six months need careful review. Kidney and liver function affect dosing. Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors like ketoconazole limit as-needed use to 10 mg once every 72 hours. Your provider also asks whether you can handle moderate exertion without chest pain.

    Tadalafil Compared to Sildenafil and Other Options in Petaluma

    Sildenafil works for four to five hours and is slowed by a heavy meal. Tadalafil lasts up to 36 hours and food barely affects it. Compounded chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets often contain tadalafil too, but compounded versions are not FDA-approved, while generic tadalafil tablets are. Tadalafil causes more back aches. Your provider weighs those trade-offs with you.
