    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Petaluma, CA

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Petaluma

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil works on a separate track. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. It works best when started early, while follicles are still producing hair, because areas that have been smooth for years rarely come back with any medicine. Postmenopausal women sometimes use finasteride off-label. It is never used in women who might become pregnant.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Petaluma

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Minoxidil comes as a twice-daily topical or a low-dose off-label tablet. One spray daily also works. The combination spray is applied to the scalp each day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. Around month six, density starts to improve. In the original finasteride trials, most men kept their hair over five years, and many saw regrowth. Full results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Petaluma

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. Most of these fade after stopping, and mood changes need prompt reporting. Scalp dryness is the usual topical issue. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention or a quicker pulse. The spray may reduce systemic side effects.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. They also review every medication and supplement, and they ask whether you plan to have children soon, since finasteride can slightly lower sperm count in some men. PSA results drop by about half. Pregnant partners should not touch broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Petaluma

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. Dutasteride + minoxidil is a stronger, off-label option, usually considered after a year on finasteride without enough change in the photos. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil by itself leaves the hormonal cause untreated.
