    How GLP-1 Microdosing Works in Petaluma

    Microdosing is a dosing strategy, not a separate drug. It means taking semaglutide or tirzepatide at doses lower than the standard labeled schedule, or increasing the dose more slowly, while the medicine still works on the same gut hormone receptors. Others notice little change. Because brand pens come in fixed doses, low-dose plans often use compounded medication, which is not FDA-approved.

    Who GLP-1 Microdosing Is For

    Three groups ask about it most. The typical candidates had trouble with nausea on standard doses, prefer a gentler start or are trying to hold onto weight they already lost. It is not a shortcut. Anti-aging and anti-inflammation claims lack solid research behind them.

    Low-Dose Schedule in Petaluma

    There is no single standard schedule. Providers often start below the usual first dose, such as a fraction of the 0.25 mg semaglutide or 2.5 mg tirzepatide starting dose, and increase in smaller steps than the label calls for. Some split the weekly amount. Never adjust the dose on your own.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Early weeks vary a lot between people. Some notice less food noise and earlier fullness within the first two weeks, while others notice almost nothing at a very low dose and need a small increase to feel an effect. Nausea is usually milder. Expect modest, gradual change.

    Side Effects in Petaluma

    Lower doses reduce side effects but do not erase them. Nausea, constipation, diarrhea, reflux and reduced appetite are still the most common, especially in the days after any dose increase, even a small one. Serious risks still apply. Pancreatitis, gallbladder problems and the thyroid C-cell tumor warning from rodent studies apply at any dose.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening is the same as for full doses. The review covers BMI, weight history, diagnoses, medications and earlier GLP-1 use, with questions about the dose you reached and what made you stop. Thyroid cancer history rules it out. Pregnancy, pancreatitis, gastroparesis and eating disorders all need careful review.

    Microdosing Compared With Standard GLP-1 Treatment in Petaluma

    Standard dosing has the trial data. The STEP and SURMOUNT trials tested full doses and reported average losses near 15 to 20 percent, and nothing comparable exists for low-dose plans. Expect smaller average results. Some people use a low dose for maintenance after reaching a goal.
