    How Women's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Petaluma, CA

    How Women's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Petaluma

    Female pattern hair loss widens the part and thins the top while the front hairline usually stays put. Genetics and follicle sensitivity to androgens drive it, and the follicles slowly shrink until they produce finer, shorter hairs that show more scalp under bright light. Minoxidil helps follicles stay in the growth phase longer. Off-label spironolactone dampens androgen effects on hair.

    Who Women's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult women with gradual thinning, a widening part or less volume on top. Women who noticed shedding two to four months after childbirth, surgery, illness or a big stress may also benefit from a review, though that type often settles on its own within six months. Patchy loss is different. Sudden bald patches, scarring or scalp pain need an in-person exam. Your photos help sort it out.

    Dosing and Schedule in Petaluma

    Topical minoxidil is FDA-approved for women as a 5 percent foam once a day or a 2 percent solution twice a day. Some women prefer off-label oral minoxidil at a low daily dose instead of a topical. Spironolactone is a daily tablet that is increased over several months. Topical blends are applied daily.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some extra shedding in months one and two. It is usually temporary, and it happens because minoxidil pushes resting hairs out so new growing hairs can replace them, which feels backward but is a normal sign. By months three to six, shedding slows and new growth may appear along the part. Fuller results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Women's Treatment in Petaluma

    Scalp itch, dryness and fine facial hair, if the liquid drips onto the forehead or cheeks, are the usual topical minoxidil complaints. Low-dose oral minoxidil sometimes causes unwanted hair growth or fluid retention. Spotting and breast soreness are common with spironolactone. It can also raise potassium. Tell your provider about a racing heart.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Pregnancy, breastfeeding and plans to conceive come first in the review. Several hair loss medicines are unsafe in pregnancy, so contraception is confirmed first. Kidney function and blood pressure get checked. Labs for thyroid, ferritin and vitamin D may be suggested.

    How Women's Options Compare in Petaluma

    Minoxidil is the base of most plans and the main FDA-approved option for women. Women with signs of excess androgens, like adult acne, often add off-label spironolactone. Minoxidil with GHK-Cu is a compounded topical that is not FDA-approved. Finasteride is mostly kept for after menopause.
